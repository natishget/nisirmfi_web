# Nisir Microfinance Policies & Terms

## Digital Saving Terms

Nisir provides digital savings services through mobile platforms.

### Eligibility

Customers must:

- Be at least 18 years old.
- Accept the terms and conditions.
- Have an active digital wallet account where applicable.

## Deposit Limits

- Minimum Deposit: 5 ETB
- Maximum Deposit: 100,000 ETB

## Interest

- Interest is calculated based on minimum balance.
- Interest is paid monthly.
- Applicable taxes may be deducted according to Ethiopian regulations.

## Data Privacy and Disclosure

Nisir may disclose customer information only when required by:

- National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE)
- Courts of law
- Authorized government institutions
- Regulatory bodies

## Security Policy

Customers are responsible for:

- Keeping PINs confidential.
- Protecting mobile devices and SIM cards.
- Reporting loss or theft immediately.
- Regularly changing account PINs.

## Service Suspension

Nisir reserves the right to suspend or terminate services in cases of:

- Fraud
- Abuse of services
- Violation of terms and conditions
- Regulatory requirements

## Amendments

Nisir may update terms, fees, and service conditions in accordance with:

- National Bank of Ethiopia regulations
- Internal operational policies

Customers will be informed before significant changes are implemented.

## Complaints Handling

Customers may submit complaints through:

- Branch visits
- Telephone support
- Email
- Written communication

Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the National Bank of Ethiopia.

## Governing Law

All services and agreements are governed by the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia.
