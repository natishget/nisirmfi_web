# Nisir Microfinance Services

## Savings Solutions

Nisir offers various savings products designed to meet different customer needs.

### Available Savings Products

- Regular Savings
- Time Deposit Savings
- Customer-Based Savings
- Women Savings
- Child Savings
- Elder Savings
- Disability Savings
- Digital Savings Products

## Credit Services

Nisir provides loan products for individuals, entrepreneurs, and businesses.

### Loan Products

#### SME Loan

Designed for small and medium enterprises with repayment options based on business cash flow.

#### Micro Business Loan

Supports micro-business expansion and development.

#### Housing Loan

Provides financing for residential housing needs.

#### Car Loan

Supports vehicle purchases.

#### Bridge Loan

Short-term financing solution.

#### Entrepreneurship Loan

Supports startup and business growth initiatives.

#### Consumption Loan

Provides financing for personal needs such as:

- Asset acquisition
- School fees
- Household expenses

## Digital Financial Services

### Digital Saving

Customers can save money using mobile applications without visiting branches.

Features:

- Minimum deposit: 5 ETB
- Interest-bearing accounts
- Monthly interest calculation
- Mobile-based account management

### Digital Credit Products

- Salary Advance Loan
- Instant Digital Loan
- Invoice Financing Loan
- Overdraft Loan
- Digital Bridge Loan
