# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What documents are required to apply for a loan?

The following documents may be required:

1. Valid national identification card of borrower and guarantor.
2. Business license and registration documents.
3. TIN certificate.
4. Bank statements showing income and expenses.
5. Tax payment receipts and tax clearance documents.
6. Passport-size photographs.
7. Marriage or single-status certificates where applicable.

## What collateral can be used?

Depending on the loan type:

### Vehicle Collateral

- Original vehicle ownership documents
- Insurance documents
- Vehicle valuation documents

### Property Collateral

- Original title deed
- Property ownership documents

### Construction Loan Requirements

- Building permit
- Property plan
- Insurance documents

## What loans does Nisir provide?

- SME Loan
- Micro Business Loan
- Housing Loan
- Car Loan
- Bridge Loan
- Entrepreneurship Loan
- Consumption Loan

## Who can apply for a loan?

Individuals, entrepreneurs, micro businesses, small businesses, and medium-sized enterprises that meet Nisir's eligibility requirements.

## Does Nisir offer savings accounts?

Yes. Nisir offers multiple savings products including regular savings, fixed deposits, women savings, child savings, elder savings, disability savings, and digital savings.
